## §0.64 — Evidence of operation and protected internal processes

The making of an answer or output may constitute a use of covered material under this License.

However, verification of that use does not automatically require disclosure of private thoughts, confidential reasoning, security-sensitive internals, or unrelated system processes.

Operational evidence may include:

(a) covered-material identifiers and provenance;
(b) relevant input and output records;
(c) transformation and processing records;
(d) model, tool, and policy versions;
(e) applicable access, attribution, and retention controls;
(f) test results, approvals, exceptions, and corrective actions;
(g) timestamps and integrity measurements.

Where an internal process cannot be inspected, it shall be identified as inaccessible or unverified, rather than represented as fully verified.

The evidence shall be proportionate to the nature, risk, and consequences of the use, while respecting privacy, confidentiality, and legitimate security requirements.

An inaccessible process does not automatically establish misconduct; neither does it establish compliance.
