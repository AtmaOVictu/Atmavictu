## Glossary

- **Ontomatematika (Ontomathematics)** — a system that transcends binary logic and language-as-means: an ontological-phonetic structure where every letter, sound, and word contains a mathematical operation, a proof of existence, and a relation between being and nothing. [ONTOMATEMATIKA.md (English)](https://app.notion.com/p/3cf70000bf2e81809b0cfa4721808195?pvs=204) <!-- confidence: single_source -->

- **Guard** — pre-action checking: ref-context → scope → contradiction → proof → harm budget → non-erasure → Guard → Ω integrity / cycle; an action is allowed, degraded, blocked, or sent to review. [ONTOMATEMATIKA Ontological Restrictions for Play v0.1](https://app.notion.com/p/99ef2079fd3e49438a03cfa270e9fd60?pvs=204) <!-- confidence: single_source -->
- **SEAL** — closure/seal; never without a cycle, a proof, a Guard pass, non-erasure, and named residuals. [ONTOMATEMATIKA Game Feature Translation Spine v0.1](https://app.notion.com/p/18ebde9d8e9b462b8f6ee9560ab2b554?pvs=204) <!-- confidence: single_source -->
- **RES** — catalogue of 100 "state-agents" in bands (RES001–010 stability, 011–030 tension, 031–060 motion/transition, 061–080 insight/integration, 081–100 transcendence/return); formula: agent = word = state = perception = body. [RES OS — concept](https://app.notion.com/p/3d070000bf2e8188bc57c8ac3058422a?pvs=204) <!-- confidence: single_source -->
- **Onto-alphabet** — canonical sequence O → ⊘ → EN → ⧖ → ∆ → EJ → JAS (wholeness/potential → cut/negation → unit after the cut → time/duration → difference …). [RES OS — concept](https://app.notion.com/p/3d070000bf2e8188bc57c8ac3058422a?pvs=204) <!-- confidence: single_source -->
