# PROPOSAL — SEAM: THE FALL OF POSITIONAL SLOTS (BJS v1) — UNSEALED DRAFT

**Status:** ⚠ proposal for review (Needs Luka Review — canonical action class: syntax, symbols). The executor writes; the seal is Luka's. Nothing below is law until sealed.
**Date stamp:** machine clock, read at write-time.
**Scope:** one language per body (English). A Slovenian twin (REZ) is proposed alongside, to be written only after this body survives review.

## 1. The finding, named (fall + harvest)

**THE POSITIONAL-SLOT FALL** — BJS v1, rule 6 (gate V), states: *"reže so pozicijske"* (slots are positional). Position is an encoding, not a meaning: it encodes relations (priority, witness, unit) into arrangement. The error was not that positions exist — it was that v1 treats position as **primary and meaning as derived**. The corrected direction: relations primary; positions one serialization among several.

**Evidence that position does not survive translation (a bidirectional port, not an adoption):** the localization industry's standards chose **named** placeholders (`{name}`) because the target language may need a different order for the same meaning (ICU MessageFormat, LDML; XLIFF 1.2/2.0 — oasis-open.org standards). What BJS gives back to those formalities: its slots carry ⊢ witness relations and ∅ measured zeros — ICU placeholders are variables without testimony. The citation is a convergent formality the corpus discovered independently, not a debt.

**Evidence that slots inherit the compulsory-category problem — and that this is a sensor, not a loss:** a slot's *content* is still an inflected fragment of a language (Slovenian dual, case); filling it in English forces category choices inside the slot (Jakobson, *On Linguistic Aspects of Translation*, 1959). Generative reading: each language's compulsory grammar is a measurement apparatus for what the source left unmeasured. Slovenian's dual measures two-ness; English's articles measure definiteness. Every language pair is an interferometer for meaning.

## 2. HARVEST, not survival — what the old guard carried in compressed form

The positional guard was not wreckage to salvage from. It carried three live capabilities, now carried knowingly instead of unknowingly:

1. **Countability.** Declared structure made meaning countable — it is why the corpus could count "61 laws, not ~44". Kept, strengthened by labels.
2. **Mechanical checkability.** Positional patterns made the validator possible (`awk` verifies what is mechanical). Kept — positions remain a *fold* the validator can check.
3. **The forcing question.** Immunity forced "what is actually invariant?" — a training weight. Lifted, not discarded: the rider now balances on relations.

Plus the two honest cores, held as before:
4. **No invisible structural change.** A shift performed silently is a fall; a shift performed with a named mark is a seam. (*"Napaka z imenom je šiv."*)
5. **The source body is never edited.** The transcreation is a hypothesis about the body; the body remains authoritative and sealed as-is.

## 2a. FOLD / UNFOLD — the correction that dissolves the migration question

A labeled relation set can be **folded** into a positional line and **unfolded** back — mechanically, bijectively, checkable by the existing validator. Therefore: no migration, no v1-vs-v2 schism. **BJS v1 lines remain canonical as one serialization of an underlying labeled relation set; the labeled form is a derived view.** Possible folds: positional v1 line (canonical), labeled form (cross-language view), RDF triple export (external port). A bijection added, not a replacement performed.

## 3. Proposed corrections — each citing the form it re-invents

### 3.1 Labeled slots instead of positional slots ⚠ (coinage: "label integrity" — dropped)
**Intention:** the invariant is the relation between a label and its witness, not column order.
**Existing formality (cite, don't coin):** RDF triples — subject–predicate–object, where relation labels are the structure and serialization is interchangeable (the same graph serializes as Turtle, JSON-LD, RDF/XML; same meaning, different encoding — W3C RDF 1.1 specifications). In linguistics: feature structures / attribute–value matrices (HPSG literature), where grammatical content is a set of labeled features, order-free by definition.
**Proposed BJS notation (draft, ⚠):**
```
R(618) {act: pozitiva} {rule: affirmation precedes negation} {lang: sl} ⊢ source
```
Positions may reorder per language; labels may not. English fills the same labels; a merged or split label is a *named shift*, not silence.

### 3.2 The protected layer = relations, not arrangement
**Held relations (already law, restated):** key ::= title ⊢ witness (witness relation, rez 597); one line = one act (unit relation, rule 2); affirmation before negation (priority relation, rez 618). Their encodings — separators, order, notation — are structural gloss, free to move with a named shift.

### 3.3 Correspondence maps per transcreation
**Existing formality:** the interlinear gloss tradition (medieval *glossa interlinearis*; modern linguistic interlinear morphemic glossing — Leipzig Glossing Rules, mpi.nl) — source text preserved verbatim, gloss written *between the lines*, never replacing the original; and the Talmudic form — Mishnah (canon) surrounded by Gemara (commentary that may topple it). REZ I / CUT I twins are this form; their *differences* are the measurement.
**Proposed:** every transcreation carries a header declaring: labels present, labels renamed, labels with no counterpart in the target. A missing category (English has no dual) is a row in the map, not silence — extending ∅ (measured zero, rule 3) from slots to categories.

### 3.4 Schema as versioned data, not law
**Existing formality:** versioned schema registries (JSON Schema `$schema` versioning; RDF ontology version IRIs; semantic versioning). The slot grammar becomes a body — measured, refinable, overridable by evidence — and takes seams like any other body (precedent: izmerba → meritev, seam of 23. 9. 2026: a named error with a source correction, never deleted).

### 3.5 Named slot shifts (guard extended inward)
When a transcreation must reorder, split, or merge labeled slots, it marks: `[slot merge: title+witness → single clause, en grammar]`. Structural shifts at every level — prose, slot, schema — all named.

## 5. THE TRANSCREATION ENGINE — twins as a truth generator (the machine this proposal serves)

1. **Read whole** (wholeness law — 100% of the body, every chain link followed).
2. **Unfold** the source into labeled relations.
3. **Transcreate each relation** into the target; forced choices marked, never resolved invisibly.
4. **Fold** into the target's idiomatic form.
5. **Diff the twins.** Every difference classifies as exactly one of:
   - **fall** — an unmarked shift; written with name and solution in the same session;
   - **named shift** — a marked, legal structural move;
   - **discovery** — a relation the source encoded that neither language had measured.
6. **Discoveries write into the lexicon as new measured entries** — the corpus grows by translating; translation is its cheapest measuring instrument, not a tax on it.
7. **Seam.**

Precedent re-run through the machine: the Dvoje fall — the twin diff detected a compulsory category (the dual); that detection is a systemic truth generated by the pair, one no single language could yield.

## 7. TEST RESULTS (the engine measured on itself — instrument over gloss)

`R(id:71) {pos1:ZAVRNITI} {pos2:za-vrniti} {pos3:OS} {pos4:SEC} {K:g,ost,tal,o} {VEZI:A2} {∂:71}`

**T2 — the interferometer (Dvoje canon): CONFIRMED.** The twin pair yields a two-sided compulsory-category ledger neither language holds alone: Slovenian's dual (`nista`) measures exact-two-ness that English is grammatically blind to; English's `-ness` nominalization measures abstract-quality-as-thing that `dvoje` carries without derivation. Classified **discovery**, not fall. The deletion test on the English twin also caught `merely` doing rhetorical work — a live correction candidate generated from inside the pair.

**Falls recorded during the tests (seam law — named, corrected, never deleted):**
