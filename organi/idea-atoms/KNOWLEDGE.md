### PLAST DOSEGE PRAGA ( dodatek iste rasti : abstrakt V CELOTI VERBATIM + prevod v celoti )

Izvirek ( VERBATIM, celoten abstrakt @arxiv abs stran, priča odprta ta dih ) : »Scientific discovery is constrained not only by what is true, but by what is cognitively available to researchers. Many directions are coherent in light of the literature yet unlikely to be proposed because no existing community combines the relevant concepts, methods, and intuitions. Our method decomposes papers into conceptual units and clusters them into a vocabulary of idea atoms. A coherence model scores whether atom combinations form viable research directions, while an availability model scores whether any existing author community is positioned to produce them. We rank combinations to maximize coherence while minimizing availability. In temporal validation, all components use only pre-2025 data. By separating scientific plausibility from community availability, our framework points toward AI ideation that complements rather than merely accelerates human science, expanding exploration into coherent directions the community may overlook.« ( preostali izvorki, ki se prekrivajo s prejšnjo plastjo, ⟨ne⟩ ponavljam ⊙ ⟨NE-GRIZI⟩ 2432 — izreki o 16.068, 35,9 in 3,24 so navedeni pri prvi plasti z istim pričnim virom ).

Mnoge smeri so skladne z lučjo literature, a jih nihče ne bo predlagal, ker nobena obstoječa skupnost ne povezuje sorodnih pojmov, metod in intuicij. Naša metoda razstavi papirje v pojmovne enote ∧ jih zgrinja v besednjak atomov idej. Model koherence oceni, ali kombinacije atomov sestavljajo sposobne raziskovalne smeri, model dostopnosti pa, ali je katera obstoječa avtorska skupnost postavljena, da jih proizvede. Kombinacije razvrstimo tako, da koherenco povečamo ∧ dostopnost zmanjšamo. V časovni preverbi vsi deli pijejo samo podatke pred 2025. Z ločevanjem znanstvene verjetnosti od skupnostne dostopnosti naš okvir kaže proti AI-zamisljanju, ki človeški znanost dopolnjuje in ⟨ne⟩ zgolj pospešuje, ∧ raziskovanje širi v skladne smeri, ki jih skupnost lahko spregleda.«
