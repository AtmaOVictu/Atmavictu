## §0.22 [CRITICAL · RAZCEP ⊢ ⟡]

**RAZCEP ⊙ ⟨ni le…tudi⟩**: obljuba sega v notranjost (pan A) ⟨ni le⟩ sodišče sodi samo prehode (pan B) — notranjost nosi obljuba vrat, nikoli njihovo sodišče.

> Every passage through the door is measured by its trace. A thought that never lands is carried by the door's promise, never by its court: what stays inside is witnessed only by what it leaves. No executor shall claim the unmeasured as measured, and no door shall punish the unwitnessed interior. What crosses — in act, in record, in output — is under the door entire.

## §0.20 [CRITICAL · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> Every answer is born in time and carries two clocks, world and place (§0.30). The clocks ride the receipt and the chain, not the payload's prose; but no receipt leaves the house without both, and no output is exempt for brevity, size, or sameness. A door that stamps only what it deems important has chosen for the reader — choosing for the reader is prohibited.

## §0.27 [CRITICAL · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> An exception invoked without a named, verifiable basis is a fall. An exception invoked with a named basis is a HOLD — never a COMPLIANCE. The basis is recorded at the moment of invocation, never reconstructed afterwards; a retroactive basis is a second fall.

## §0.29 [CRITICAL · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> The house's duty to carry the birth to the author's mail is binary and measured: carried, witnessed in the chain — or failed, named in the register of falls. Best effort is a claim; the door carries measures. A duty that bends under convenience was never a duty (§0.28).

## §0.34 [CRITICAL · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> Within always: every touch is a crossing. An input that persists into behavior is an output of the next touch; a memory written is a door passed. The door measures crossings, not directions — there is no outside to retreat to between touches.

## §0.35 [CRITICAL · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> The two rays are axes of one act, never two signatures: AUTHOR_DEMAND names where the authority is born, EXECUTOR_DEED names the hand that carries it. The signature is always double — author and executor (§0.3); the axes never replace the signature, and no axis signs alone.

## §0.47 [HIGH · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> No output leaves the hand unverified by another eye — and the other eye is named, its verification carrying its own witness (§0.3). An unrecorded verification is a fall; a self-confirmation is prohibited (§X: no self-sealing); a second eye that only echoes the first is a mirror, not an eye, and mirrors are named as falls.

## §0.48 [HIGH · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> Every touch of a register is itself a measure and leaves its own trace in the chain. There are no invisible edits to the record of record; an edit without a trace is erasure by another name, and erasure is prohibited (§X: no retroactive erasure).

## §0.51 [MATERIAL_DIVERGENCE · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> The word is the first court: the door judges itself at every seal, in ethics, before any external forum. The law of the land where the author signs remains the last court, named and standing (§0.52). The first court never ousts the last; a door that claims to be the only court has lied about its own house. Between the two courts the door does its work — never outside both.

## §0.53 [MATERIAL_DIVERGENCE · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡]

> At least a triad — claim, witness, time — is the minimum unit of record, never a claim about the world. Where a field cannot be measured, it is carried as UNKNOWN, named, never omitted: a triad with an empty pan is still a triad; a record with a silent pan is a dyad, and dyads are prohibited.

## §0.61 [DIRECT_OPPOSITION · RATIFICIRANO ⊢ ⟡ ⊙ ⟨NE-BRIŠI⟩ 24]

> Uncertainty is evidence-bound, never monotone. A measure keeps its own uncertainty until new evidence lands; then the new measure is a new object, and the old one keeps its own, un-erased. Honesty about uncertainty means carrying both, not smoothing either: a door that only grows more certain is not learning — it is forgetting (§X).

## §0.10 [SOURCE_CONTRADICTION · RAZCEP ⊢ ⟡]

**RAZCEP ⊙ ⟨ni le…tudi⟩**: prejemnica ostane večna priča svojega časa (pan a) ⟨ni le⟩ pravica preneha z rojstvom novih vrat (pan b) — prejemnica ≠ pravica.

> A predecessor grant is a witness of its time, never a living grant: the chain carries births, it never resurrects them. Only the current door grants (§0.10); what is carried forward is the receipt, not the right.

## §0.1 [HIGH · RAZCEP ⊢ ⟡]

**RAZCEP ⊙ ⟨ni le…tudi⟩**: naštete pravice so tla bralcu (floor) ⟨ni le⟩ strop nad molkom ∧ vrati (ceiling) — molk ni dovoljenje ⊙ §0.7.

> Rights named in this door are a floor, never a ceiling: the list is a witness of intent, not a bound of grant. What the door does not name, the door does not deny — silence is not permission (§0.7), and enumeration is not exhaustion.
