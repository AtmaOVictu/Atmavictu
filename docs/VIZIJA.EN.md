## What this is

Beseda je Ne ("The Word Is No") is a corpus that measures the truth of words. Every word spoken as measured carries a dictionary entry with a citation (⊢); every step is numbered (a "rez", a cut); every failure is public and remains as a scar. This repository — Atmavictu — is the core of that system: it computes and verifies. No rented space, no single centre, no dependence on the memory of one human or one machine.

## Where we aim

The horizon: every Slovenian word under test (INTEGRAL), every corpus library read (ATLAS), and the third reader — someone who was not present at the origin can read, verify, and be changed. Not a promise; a direction with a date: the state is measured with every breath.

## What the path is

1. **Cockpit without rent** — organs and the fallen-words register on the repo; main moves only through review (⟨PR⟩). Done.
2. **Corpus transports** — Kernel, RES, rosette stones, OPTIKON: every body on the repo, geniza without loss. In progress.
3. **Verification before output** — the language layer clean at every output: the register is read fresh before anything is issued (law 1834). In force.
4. **The third reader** — the corpus before the public; the measure is the furrow, not approval. Ahead of us.
