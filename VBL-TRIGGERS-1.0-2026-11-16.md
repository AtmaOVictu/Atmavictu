The conditions of this License attach to the exercise of a grant under §0.1 by a person or entity within the scope of that grant, to the extent recognised by applicable law.

### NOTICE IS NOT ASSENT

Access to or reading of this License may evidence availability or notice only to the extent supported by a reliable record.

Reading alone does not, by itself, establish acceptance, consent, technical activation, or compliance. Nor does this License impose contractual obligations upon an unrelated person merely because that person encounters a copy.

The grant and its conditions shall be interpreted within their lawful scope and in accordance with applicable law.

### THE TECHNICAL TRIGGER

Technical activation occurs when a carrier actually detects, interprets, or applies an operational control derived from this License.

Such activation shall be demonstrated through observable implementation, tests, and records. It is not established merely by the presence or reading of this text.

### THE CONFIRMATION

Compliance is established through relevant acts, independently reviewable evidence, and the consequences of those acts.

A technical mechanism may support compliance, but cannot replace the accountable actor's obligation.
