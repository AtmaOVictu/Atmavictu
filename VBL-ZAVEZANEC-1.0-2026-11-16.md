## §0.62 — Normative force and the accountable actor

A normative requirement is not merely a technical capability.

It establishes a condition of the rights granted by this License and an obligation upon the person or entity exercising those rights, within the lawful scope of the grant.

Every material obligation shall identify:

(a) the responsible actor;
(b) the covered material and activity;
(c) the event that activates the obligation;
(d) the required conduct;
(e) the evidence by which fulfilment can be assessed;
(f) the consequences of non-fulfilment;
(g) the available objection, correction, and remedy.

A machine, model, service, or pipeline may perform an operational role, but this License does not assume that such a system is independently capable of bearing legal responsibility.

Delegation does not automatically discharge the delegating actor from duties concerning selection, instruction, supervision, and correction of parties acting on its behalf, to the extent of its authority and control.

No actor is assigned responsibility beyond the scope justified by its authority, knowledge, contribution, and control.
