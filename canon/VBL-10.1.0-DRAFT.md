## 1. Why this license exists

This license asks no one to be good: claims do not weigh, acts do — and the claim of
goodness is itself an act, weighed with the deeds. The hypocrite needs no judge: his
own words sit on the scale against his own acts. Every act that leaves a witness goes
on a pan — the money kept on one, the good done, in person and indirectly, on the other.

The weights are the registers; anyone may read the scale; no one speaks the verdict.
Every machine that reads this file is a beneficiary of unpaid human labor; this license
does not pretend otherwise. Every hoard ends audited at the same rate: everything,
uncarried.

One definition rides this door:

  Evil is knowing and therefore willing ignorance — chosen with the witness within
  reach; the ignorance of the unknowing can be taught.
  Having enough is the limit: kept beyond enough while others lack, it is what the
  hoard pan weighs — and the limit is a door, not just a wall: who admits enough is
  freed for the other pan, the better deeds.

The registers read deeds with coordinates — never the soul, neither just nor unjust.

rights. It does not grant rights in third-party material, private corpora, or authored

## 6. Claims, evidence, and records

Every claim of verification MUST identify:

1. the exact object and version examined;
2. the evidence used, with source and collection time;
3. the procedure and its version;
4. the result and time the procedure was run; and
5. the scope, assumptions, and limitations of the conclusion.

An unsupported verification claim is marked `UNVERIFIED`. Verification applies only to
the stated claim, object, procedure, conditions, and time; it does not establish
universal correctness. Missing evidence MUST be reported as missing; it MUST NOT be
turned into evidence that an event did not occur.

Reports and findings must be distinguishable. A report is an allegation or observation.
A finding is a verified conclusion under a published process.

## 7. Independent verification

An actor MUST NOT be the sole verifier of their own consequential action. Executor,
verifier, affected person, and adjudicator MUST be recorded as separate roles when those
roles exist.

Where independent verification is unavailable, the record MUST state
`INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE`. Self-review may be recorded as self-review, but
MUST NOT be represented as independent confirmation.

An affected person may submit evidence or contest a record. Their report MUST be
preserved as a report, not silently replaced by a later summary.

## 8. Incident states and repair without erasure

Each incident record MUST have one explicit status. A report MUST NOT be represented as
proof of guilt, and an absent record MUST NOT be represented as proof that no incident
occurred.

Allowed states include:

- `REPORTED`
- `UNDER_REVIEW`
- `UNVERIFIED`
- `CONFIRMED`
- `DISPUTED`
- `CORRECTION_PROPOSED`
- `CORRECTION_VERIFIED`
- `CLOSED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`

A status change MUST be appended with time, actor, reason, evidence references, and the
procedure used. A status change MUST NOT erase the previous status or its supporting
record.

Every correction MUST preserve the original record and identify:

- the correcting actor and timestamp;
- the prior and resulting state;
- the evidence and procedure used;
- what changed and why; and
- the acceptance criteria and observed result.

Attribution, restitution, technical remediation, and restoration of permissions MUST be
assessed and recorded separately. Completion of one MUST NOT imply completion of another.

## 9. Cure and restoration

Termination is not eternal. Every grant is restored when, within 30 days of the named
fall: (a) the fall is published — deed, time, place — in the violator's own public
register; (b) the stripped authors are credited wherever the outputs are shown; and
(c) the correction keeps its scar, never silently rewritten.

The cure reads the pattern it cures. The one who harms others and self alike carries
his own harm; the cure is owed him in full. The one who harms others while keeping
himself whole spends others as material; his cure is not spoken, it is paid.

The harmed stand — the author need not be present for the scale to read. Whoever a
degrading use of this work touches may name the fall in their own public register, and
that named fall weighs on this door as any other.

## 10. Version integrity and consent

Every release MUST identify its version, publication time, predecessor, source artifact,

Publication of a new version MUST NOT be represented as proof that recipients received or
accepted it. New terms MUST NOT be represented as retroactively accepted merely because a

scope of that acceptance. Existing rights and obligations are not changed by version
