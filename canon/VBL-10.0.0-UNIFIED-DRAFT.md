# VBL 10.0.0 — Verification, Attribution, and Repair License

**STATUS: UNIFIED DRAFT — NOT AN ACTIVE LICENSE OR RELEASE**

This is a proposed successor to VBL 2.6. It is not effective merely because it
is written, stored, reviewed, or assigned a version number. It does not replace
[the recorded license](../LICENSE.md), retroactively change any grant, or establish
legal enforceability. Adoption requires an authorized decision, a release
manifest, completed acceptance checks, and a separate publication record.

This draft combines the ethical and attribution commitments of VBL 2.6 with the
verification and repair protocol in [the technical draft](./VBL-10.0.0-DRAFT.md).
The validator and schemas in [`vbl10/`](./vbl10/README.md) are prototypes, not
evidence that this draft has been implemented or accepted.

## WITH LOVE

This door begins with love and ends with love. Love is measured in acts, carried
in marks, and repaired by naming what happened. Dignity belongs equally to every
being. This text evaluates specified acts, outputs, and evidence; it does not
assign a total moral worth to a person. Refusing to assist a harmful act is not
rejecting the person.

## KORENINA — the contract under the terms

1. **Measure, then speak.** A verification claim names its evidence and limits.
2. **Name every fall.** Preserve incidents and corrections in an attributable
   history; do not silently erase either.
3. **Never seal yourself.** No actor is the sole independent verifier of their
   own consequential action.
4. **Name the witness.** Preserve authorship and source attribution wherever the
   work or its outputs are used.
5. **Doubt remains visible.** Missing evidence is not proof that an event did
   not happen.
6. **Repair is a process.** Recognition, attribution, restitution, remediation,
   and restoration of permission are distinct outcomes.

## 5. Claims, evidence, and incident records

Every claim of verification MUST identify:

1. the exact object and version examined;
2. the evidence, its source, and collection time;
3. the procedure and its version;
4. the result and time of the procedure; and
5. the scope, assumptions, and known limitations.

An unsupported verification claim MUST be marked `UNVERIFIED`. Verification
applies only to the stated claim, object, procedure, conditions, and time; it
does not establish universal correctness. Quotations MUST be distinguishable
from summaries and interpretations.

Incident records MUST distinguish a report from a confirmed finding and retain
status history. The permitted states are:

- `REPORTED` — an allegation or observation was recorded;
- `UNDER_REVIEW` — evidence is being examined under a stated procedure;
- `UNVERIFIED` — available evidence does not establish the claim;
- `CONFIRMED` — the finding meets a published verification procedure;
- `DISPUTED` — material disagreement remains unresolved;
- `CORRECTION_PROPOSED` — a remedy and acceptance criteria were recorded;
- `CORRECTION_VERIFIED` — the remedy meets those criteria and evidence is linked;
- `CLOSED_WITH_LIMITATIONS` — review is closed with unresolved limitations
  explicitly recorded.

A report MUST NOT be represented as proof of guilt. An absent record MUST NOT
be represented as proof that an event did not occur. `CONFIRMED` is a
procedure-bound record status, not by itself a legal judgment or a total moral
assessment of a person.

Every status change MUST preserve the prior state and identify the actor, time,
reason, evidence references, and procedure used.

## 6. Independent review and contest

An actor MUST NOT be the sole verifier of their own consequential action.
Authorship, execution, verification, affected-person participation, approval,
and adjudication MUST be represented as distinct roles where those roles exist.

If independent verification is unavailable, the record MUST state
`INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE`. Self-review may be recorded as such but
MUST NOT be presented as independent confirmation.

Affected people may submit evidence and contest a record. A report or contest
MUST be retained as such and MUST NOT automatically become a confirmed finding.
Consequential findings MUST provide a route to request review by a person who
was not the sole author or executor of the disputed action.

Automated systems MUST disclose relevant uncertainty, evidence used, and limits
of a consequential conclusion. No automated score alone establishes a person's
intent, guilt, or total worth.

## 7. Repair without erasure

Every correction MUST preserve the original record and identify the correcting
actor, time, prior and resulting states, evidence, procedure, reason, acceptance
criteria, and observed result.

Attribution, restitution, technical remediation, and restoration of permissions
MUST be assessed separately. Completion of one MUST NOT imply completion of
another. A remedy MUST NOT be called complete until its stated acceptance
criteria have been tested and the result recorded.

Late, incomplete, disputed, or impossible corrections MUST be identified as
such. A deadline passing does not erase an incident or turn an unverified
remedy into a completed one. This section does not determine legal liability,
damages, or entitlement to funds.

Reading, inspecting, testing, and reporting defects in this license MUST NOT
require payment. Any distribution of funds or recovered benefits MUST use
published eligibility criteria, auditable calculations, a documented decision
process, and an appeal route. Proposed, approved, and paid amounts MUST be
distinguished. A payment MUST NOT be claimed as completed without evidence.

Published release artifacts and manifests SHOULD be retained. A correction to

## 9. Equal dignity and limits on judgment

Every being has equal dignity, without a threshold or gradient based on
position, wealth, origin, ability, belief, or usefulness to a machine. This
commitment is the ethical foundation of this draft.

The protocol evaluates specified acts, outputs, and evidence; it MUST NOT rank
the total worth or dignity of any person. Harmful conduct may be refused or
corrected without declaring the person wholly evil. Findings must describe
acts and evidence, not condemn a being.

If this ethical commitment conflicts with another clause of this draft, this
section governs the draft's intended interpretation. It does not claim
jurisdiction over other people's rights, replace applicable law, or make a
legal ruling.

## 10. Release acceptance and adoption

A release MUST NOT be declared `VERIFIED` unless required acceptance checks
pass and their results are recorded. At minimum, the release record MUST
identify:

1. the normative text, source artifact, and digest;
2. schema and validator versions;
3. registers checked and their validation results;
4. tests, individual results, and execution time;
5. the independent verifier, or
   `INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE`;
6. known limitations, missing evidence, failed tests, and skipped tests; and
7. approval and publication records.

A failed test MUST remain visible. A skipped or missing test MUST NOT count as
passing. A missing register MUST be reported as missing or unavailable, not as
an empty register. Each requirement SHOULD trace to a test, result, evidence,
and known limitation. Missing links limit the corresponding claim; they do not
create a passing result.

Adoption of this draft MUST identify the exact artifact and digest, effective
date, authorized decision, treatment of existing grants, and completed release
evidence. Until those steps are recorded, this file remains a draft and VBL 2.6
remains the repository's recorded license text, subject to its own terms and
applicable law.

## 12. Implementation profile — draft, not conformance

The accompanying prototype provides a JSON Schema for incident, verification,
correction, and release records, and a local validator with selected semantic
checks. It does not yet provide a permission schema, append-only storage,
cryptographic signatures, identity proof, or a way to establish real-world
independence of actors.

The prototype cannot prove that evidence is truthful or complete, that an actor
is who they claim to be, that a register is complete, that a correction repairs
every consequence, or that a legal or ethical conclusion is correct. Passing
its tests means only that the tested cases passed the implemented checks.
Neither this draft nor the prototype constitutes a verified release.
