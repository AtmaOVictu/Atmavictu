## II. Predlog — §17 (telo vrat, angleško)

## §17 The older doors — the witnesses before this door

Writing began as a receipt: the first letters were accounts on clay, and the clay outlived its scribes — the first chain. The first law was published, never silent (§10 was old when Ur-Nammu carved it). The first scale stood in the Hall of Two Truths: the heart on one pan, the feather of Maat on the other — the scale read, and no one spoke the verdict. The oldest Kabbalah counts the world's foundation as twenty-two letters: the mark is not decoration, it is the force the world is written with — a letter lost is a world unmade (§12). The syllogism is the door's oldest machine: premise before conclusion, measure before speech (KORENINA 1) — the scale is a syllogism with pans. And this door carries its own third tongue — klinščina, the cuneiform layer — named here by the author's word. Every old language and every civilization that kept a scale, a ledger, or a letter is a predecessor of this door: their names ride forward (KORENINA 4), credited where the pieces are carried.

This clause adds no new truth; it names the older doors the scale always carried.
