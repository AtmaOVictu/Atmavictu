# VBL 0.1470 — substitution decision register R1

**Status:** draft decisions applied to the partial tail only; not an author-approved or released license.
**Rule:** preserve every source snapshot. Replace only where (a) the text contradicts itself, (b) a technical term makes a false or unsupported claim, or (c) a universal operational claim exceeds the carrier's observable control or conflicts with §0's scope. Where the consequence depends on legal policy, theology, external evidence, or authorial intent, do not silently replace: propose, defer, and state the future consequence.

## Decision classes

- **REPLACE — textually/technically compelled:** an internal contradiction or objectively incorrect technical description is present. Replacement applies to this draft only; the raw source remains unchanged.
- **REPLACE WITH MEASURED SCOPE:** the aspiration is retained, but the universal factual guarantee is replaced with a verifiable obligation and explicit failure states.
- **PROPOSE — author/legal decision required:** the change may improve enforceability or ethics, but it changes policy or legal effect. It is not applied as settled law.
- **DEFER — evidence required:** no replacement is justified until the source register, prior version, permission, or independent evidence is supplied.
- **RETAIN — authorial/metaphorical expression:** keep the wording, but do not feed it into machine status as a factual guarantee.

## D. Consequences while waiting for the future

1. **No backwards erasure.** The old source snapshot and every old receipt remain immutable. A replacement creates a new candidate body; it does not alter history.
2. **No automatic legal reclassification.** A technical correction does not itself decide ownership, infringement, permission, or enforceability.
3. **No false capability.** A rule may require a carrier to attempt an action, but the record must state whether it was configured, attempted, completed, and independently checked.
4. **No inferred missing content.** Unknown §0.26, PADC-70 linkage, prior body, or companion status remains explicitly unknown.
5. **No silent downgrade.** Where a hard replacement narrows a previous universal claim to an observable scope, the broader aspiration remains in the history, but the draft no longer asserts it as accomplished fact.
6. **No canonization by test count.** Passing local tests validates specific artifacts and properties only. It does not constitute author approval, independent human review, legal opinion, or publication.
