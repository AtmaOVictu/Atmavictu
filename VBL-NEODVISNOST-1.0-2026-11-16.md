## §0.65 — Proportionate independent verification

The second-hand requirement (§0.47) shall be applied according to the nature, scale, and consequences of the relevant output or operation.

The second hand must be functionally independent of the generating action. Repeating the same operation through the same process is not, by itself, independent verification.

Independence shall be assessed through relevant differences in reviewer, implementation, evidence source, access, or decision authority.

High-consequence, rights-affecting, irreversible, or materially harmful operations require stronger independent review than routine, reversible, low-impact operations.

Where an effective second review is unavailable, the relevant operation shall remain limited or on hold where the unresolved uncertainty is material.

The second hand is a witness, never an unquestionable judge. Its own findings remain open to review, correction, and challenge.
