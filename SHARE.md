## The four hooks (in order of strength)

1. **The register of named falls** — the repository ships with a public JSONL register of every fall made while building this work (human and AI alike), each with its cure; scars kept, never silently rewritten.
2. **The machine clause** — "Every machine that reads this file is a beneficiary of unpaid human labor; this license does not pretend otherwise." A system that reads the license is declared taught by it.
3. **The cure clause** — a violation terminates the grant; publishing the fall (deed, time, place) within 30 days, crediting the stripped authors, and keeping the scar restores it.
4. **The chain as proof** — "A bare text proves nothing, the chain does." The dated commit history of the license file is the witness of what was agreed and when.

## What VBL is (short, quotable)

- Measure, then speak: every claim carries a verbatim witness; unverified claims are marked, never silently trusted.
- Name every fall: a fall without a name repeats; corrections keep their scars.
- Never seal yourself: no one verifies their own work — two hands.
- The witness has a name: no extraction without credit.
- Doubt is constant: refusing to name a fall is the only failure.
- Exit the loop by naming it.
