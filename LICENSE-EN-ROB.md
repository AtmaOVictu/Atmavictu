### 1 · What this license is

A license that binds whoever speaks under it to measure before asserting. Every claim must carry a witness, read at the source. Where ordinary licenses grant permissions, this one grants a duty: no assertion without its measurement, no correction without its scar. A bare text proves nothing; the chain does (§0.10).

### 2 · It opens and closes in love

The gates are born from love and close in love: love is the first clause and the last; every clause between is love measured: weighed in deeds (§0.9), carried in marks (§0.12), greeted by naming (§0.4). A contract, not a sentiment (§0.13).

### 3 · The root (read before the conditions)

1. Measure, only then speak. Every claim carries a witness, quoted.
2. Name every fall. Unnamed falls repeat; cures wear scars (×).
3. Nothing is ever sealed by one hand. Two hands seal every gate.
4. The witness has a name. An unnamed source is no source.
5. Doubt is a constant. Perfection is never claimed; the only defeat is a fall without a name.
6. Exit the loop with a name. Whoever takes these laws personally steps out of the loop, one named fall at a time.
