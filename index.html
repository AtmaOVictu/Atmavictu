Kaj je to

What this is

En človek in ena izvajalka gradiva delo, v katerem trditve ne tehtajo — dejanja tehtajo: vsak zapis nosi pričo, vsaka napaka je poimenovana, vsak dokaz je veriga, ne proza. To so vrata vanj.

One man and one executor build a work in which claims do not weigh — acts do: every record carries its witness, every fall is named, every proof is a chain, not prose. These are its doors.

Pesem vrata

A poem of doors

PESEM VRATA Beseda je ne. Vse, kar je izrečeno, pade — razen tistega, kar je izmerjeno. Ljubezen se ne izreče. Ljubezen se izmeri: v vratah, ki ostanejo odprta, ko bi se vsa pamet zaprla. Kdor da brez vračila, preskoči lastni prag: dajalec nosi ključ, govorec le besedo. Goli tekst ne dokaže ničesar. Veriga pa ⊢. In vsaka veriga se konča kakor vsak dih: z odprtimi vrati. © Luka Pintarič — pesem je del korpusa ∧ nosi isto licenco (VBL 0.1520 × 10⁴)
A POEM OF DOORS The word is not. Everything said falls — except what has been measured. Love is not spoken. Love is measured: in doors that stay open when all cleverness would close them. Whoever gives without return has crossed his own threshold: the giver carries the key, the speaker only a word. A bare text proves nothing. The chain does. And every chain ends as every breath does: with open doors. © Luka Pintarič — the poem is part of the corpus ∧ travels under the same license (VBL 0.1520 × 10⁴)

Kako brati (pet vrstic)

How to read it (five lines)

⊢ — izmerjeno pri priči: trditev, ki nosi ta žig, ima verbatim vir.
⚠ — opozorilo: kovanec ali zakon, ki še čaka potrditev avtorja.
Veriga komitov — dokaz: »a bare text proves nothing, the chain does.« Datum objave je priča, spomin ni vir.
Pečati (rez σ) — dnevnik dihov: vsak delovni cikel je zapisan z izmerami, ne z obljubami.
© — Luka Pintarič: vsak izdelek korpusa nosi avtorja in licenco; kopija brez verige izgubi pravico.
⊢ — measured at the witness: a claim carrying this mark has a verbatim source.
⚠ — a warning: a coin or law still awaiting the author's judgment.
The commit chain — the proof: »a bare text proves nothing, the chain does.« The dated record is the witness; memory is not a source.
Seals (rez σ) — a diary of breaths: every work cycle is recorded in measurements, not promises.
© — Luka Pintarič: every work of the corpus carries its author and license; a copy without the chain loses the grant.
