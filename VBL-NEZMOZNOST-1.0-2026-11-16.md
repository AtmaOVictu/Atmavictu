## §0.63 — Capability, inability, and the duty to refrain

Every actor exercising a grant shall assess, before the relevant use, whether the technical, organisational, contractual, and evidential capabilities necessary to satisfy its material obligations are available.

The absence of a necessary capability does not constitute fulfilment.

Where a material obligation cannot be satisfied, the responsible actor shall, as appropriate:

(a) prevent the affected use from beginning;
(b) limit the use to the portion for which compliance can be demonstrated;
(c) suspend or cease the affected operation;
(d) preserve the evidence of the limitation or failure;
(e) notify affected parties where required;
(f) initiate correction and restoration before resuming the affected use.

An actor shall not represent an unavailable or untested control as operational.

An inability beyond an actor's reasonable control is not automatically intentional misconduct. Its consequences shall be assessed against foreseeability, available safeguards, actual control, knowledge, diligence, and the response taken.

The inability to perform an obligation never converts an unverified result into a verified one.
